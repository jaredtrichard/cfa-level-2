# Lesson template — provisional, not a taught lesson

Use for one manageable target; do not fill out the whole curriculum at once.

## Target and coverage

Outcome ID(s), official page/bullet, source coverage (unverified/partial/checked), and the exact portion taught. Mark gaps explicitly. Identify a useful prerequisite refresher without locking access.

## Explanation

Explain the mechanism and why it matters in the investment decision. Keep the reasoning required by the outcome; omit repetitive summaries.

## Method and assumptions

Define quantities, units, timing, applicable assumptions, exceptions, and failure cases. Record source/edition/section and errata check date.

## Worked example

Use an original case. Show calculation steps and interpretation, with figures on separate lines. Independently check the arithmetic and reconcile it to the stated assumptions. Record how it was checked.

## Independent teachback

Link to the separate prompt in practice/<topic>/; do not embed its answer here. Ask for the decision, worked method where applicable, and interpretation. Solutions live in a separate file with a source-backed rubric. Create a fresh unseen case when validation is needed; this teaching example cannot itself prove mastery.

## Improvement note

Describe the general improvement and public source basis when changing the lesson. Keep actual learner feedback/attempt evidence only in the private log. This template is a starting hypothesis; no learner calibration has occurred.
