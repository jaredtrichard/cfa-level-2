# Independent teachback: a property fund study

**Outcome:** L2-2027-QM-01-01

**Prompt version:** 1

A property fund analyst wants to understand why occupancy differs across comparable office buildings. Candidate information includes asking rent, building age, access to public transport, and employment conditions in the surrounding area.

A colleague proposes using the same study to forecast next year's occupancy before the fund acquires a building. The colleague suggests selecting whichever model most closely matches the historical observations, using next year's actual local employment growth as an input, and treating the rent coefficient as proof that cutting rent will cause higher occupancy.

Explain your response in your own words:

- Describe the investment uses of the proposed study. Distinguish understanding current differences from forecasting a later outcome. Identify the response and plausible predictors for each purpose.
- Walk through the regression process from the investment question to a decision. Explain the data checks, estimation, evaluation, validation, and possible revision you would require.
- Assess each of the colleague's proposals and explain how you would improve it.
- Explain how the study could inform the acquisition decision without making that decision automatically.

No coefficient calculation is required by this checkbox. If you use a numerical illustration, show the units and reasoning. Submit your answer in the study conversation. Do not consult the separate solution first.
