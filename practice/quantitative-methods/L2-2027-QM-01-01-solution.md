# Grading guide: property fund study

**Outcome:** L2-2027-QM-01-01

**Rubric version:** 1

**Prompt:** [Property fund study](L2-2027-QM-01-01-prompt.md)

## Expected reasoning

The first objective is to describe cross-building differences in occupancy. Occupancy is the response; rent, age, transit access, and local employment conditions are plausible candidate predictors, subject to definition, economic rationale, and data suitability. Multiple regression considers several predictors together. The learner need not use every candidate or claim that they must matter.

The second objective predicts future occupancy from information available at the decision date, or explicitly forecast inputs. It needs a clearly defined horizon and evaluation resembling that use. Current explanatory relationships do not automatically establish predictive usefulness.

A sound process defines the objective, response, predictors, observation unit, period, and form; assembles and inspects suitable data; estimates candidate models; evaluates plausibility, fit, assumptions, and unusual observations; revises where warranted; evaluates predictive performance on appropriately held-out later observations; and communicates a bounded conclusion. Equivalent terminology and sensible grouping are acceptable. Do not demand a memorized count or exact sequence where iterative reasoning is sound.

Matching historical observations closely is insufficient on its own. It does not establish later predictive accuracy or a defensible explanation. Next year's realized employment growth is unavailable at the acquisition date; using it as though known introduces future information. A forecast of employment may be used with its additional uncertainty acknowledged. A rent association does not alone prove the effect of intervening to cut rent: omitted property characteristics and reverse direction of influence can matter.

A proposed occupancy forecast or comparison can inform expected rents and cash flows, vacancy risk, further investigation, and acquisition valuation. It does not replace the entire acquisition analysis. Occupancy is bounded, so a careful learner may note model-suitability concerns; do not require advanced bounded-response modeling in this first checkbox.

## Assessment dimensions

- **Explain:** Distinguishes investment uses, identifies response versus predictors, and describes the major stages and their purposes. Incorrectly treating regression as automatic causal proof is material.
- **Apply:** Adapts the process to the property case; handles input availability, evaluation, and bounded decision use. Mere repetition of process labels without applying them is insufficient.
- **Calculate:** Not directly required by this descriptive outcome. Do not demand estimation, hypothesis tests, or coefficient arithmetic. Independently verify any optional numerical illustration without converting an arithmetic slip into evidence of a conceptual error.

One correct answer supports independent evidence; it does not by itself confer demonstrated mastery. Fresh cases and later cold retrieval remain necessary under the study skill. Record hints and solution exposure, preserve the original answer before grading, and keep actual grading exclusively in local-data/.
