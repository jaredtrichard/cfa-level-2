## Differences requiring attention

Outside ethics, the observed textual differences are the removal of a final period in cost-of-debt outcome CF-03-02, spacing of “cash flows” in DE-01-02, and removal of a semicolon in PC-06-07. No other outcome wording differences were found outside ethics by this bounded comparison. This is not a finding that only ethics content changed.

ET-01 now addresses the conduct enforcement program and disciplinary process in its first outcome; its second outcome now refers explicitly to standard subsections. The old combined guidance module becomes separate modules for Standards I through VII. The application module moves from local ET-03 to ET-09 with the same outcome wording. No remaining old module was unmatched outside the replaced combined guidance module.

The [official change summary](https://www.cfainstitute.org/sites/default/files/curriculum-update-2027.pdf) confirms ethics updates across levels to reflect the twelfth-edition Standards of Practice Handbook and new guidance. Treat ethics as needing current substantive sources, even where application wording matches.
