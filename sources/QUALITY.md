## Provisional format calibration

The inspected [AnalystPrep present-value lesson](https://analystprep.com/study-notes/cfa-level-2/present-value-models/) pairs model explanation with suitability conditions. Its [company valuation lesson](https://analystprep.com/study-notes/cfa-level-2/estimating-a-companys-value/) uses staged forecasts and worked valuation. These support trying target → explanation → assumptions/method → worked example → independent teachback, not mandating that structure forever. Their visible reading labels are not a verified 2027 edition mapping. No learner feedback has yet calibrated the format.

The valuation page's first example lists inputs that imply first-year earnings of 0.77625, but its table prints 1.150. The independently checked computation is current sales × first-year growth factor × stated net margin. Its first-year FCFE calculation is consistent with the input-derived earnings, not that table entry. Treat the table as an unresolved provider inconsistency, not a learner error. Do not reuse its answer key unchecked. No broad audit of either page was performed.

## Before a lesson or assessment is used

- Each HTML module has one primary checkbox/outcome ID. Check every action and subpart, including required assumptions, exceptions, calculations, and interpretation. Partial coverage is a draft, not a completed module. Trace practice to its actual tested portion. Mark substantive coverage unverified, partial, or checked separately from learning state.
- Check assumptions, exceptions, timing, units, edition, and relevant errata. An unchanged heading or outcome is insufficient.
- Independently calculate numerical solutions with a calculator or simple local computation; record the check. Resolve discrepancies before grading affected work.
- Write original question variants and case narratives. Cite conceptual sources and edition/section; avoid copying proprietary problems or full textbook text.
- Keep learner prompts separate from sourced solutions/rubrics. Preserve unseen validation cases until needed and record exposure.
- Update the current lesson immediately from verified errors or useful feedback, retaining linked correction history in the log. Do not mass-author a course or relax the required reasoning.
